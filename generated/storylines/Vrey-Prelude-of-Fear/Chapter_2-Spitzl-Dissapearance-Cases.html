After another week, you finally arrive at Spitzburg! It is a small town with various farms far and wide surrounding it - except to the West, where you see many towering snowy mountains and the colourful autumn forests covering the western horizon. You easily spotted a building that stood out due to its large plaque with the word: “Rathaus” - the townhall. However, you notice that there are many people gathering around it. There’s a woman desperately trying to enter the townhall while the other townfolks are preventing her from entering it.

The woman (Elissa) has lost her son, Jordi to the phenomenon last night, and is trying to force her way to the mayor and request an action to be done.

The other villagers: But those who investigate will disappear too! We do not want to lose you or anyone anymore! Just let it pass soon…

Suddenly, the town hall doors are open. A man with brown, long hair and beard and simple blue tunic walks out from the town hall. Everyone looks at the man and everything falls silent. “Elissa, and you, representatives from Hevrion, please come in.”

The interior of the town hall feels very different from the one at Wize or Hevrion. Simple grey stone wall with minimum decorations with minimum light trickling in from the small holes between the walls. The man gestures to everyone to sit around a wooden table, before opening a small and colourful stained glass window. “Those robes surely bring memories. I am Javier, just an old alumni of Hevrion.” He pointed to a blue robe hanging on a rack behind him. And Elissa, I believe they can be the key to solving this case.”

Upon hearing that, Elissa trembles a bit, prays, shakes your hands and thanks all of you.
